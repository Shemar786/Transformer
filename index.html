Flight manual

  1. Tap a runway lane, swipe sideways, or use ← and → to steer Fax.
  2. Fly through baggage tags and fuel cells to pick them up. Dodge barriers or climb over them with ↑ or an upward swipe. A hit costs one tag.
  3. Use the trade board to turn supplies into parcels, flight kits, and eventually the Golden Ticket. Open an available trade at any time to pause the runway.
