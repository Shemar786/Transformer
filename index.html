How to play

  • Use ← and →, swipe, or tap the arrow buttons to change lanes.
  • During the farm shift, run through crops to collect them. Press ↑ or swipe up to jump over crates.
  • At night, steer the Toyota Probox toward waiting fares and away from traffic.
  • Reach J$2,500 across as many shifts as you need. Press Esc to pause.
